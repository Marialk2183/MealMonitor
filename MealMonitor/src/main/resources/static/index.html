About MealMonitor

MealMonitor is a community-driven platform designed to improve food quality and safety in educational institution canteens.

Our mission is to create transparency and accountability in campus dining facilities by enabling students, faculty, and staff to share real-time feedback, ratings, and photos of canteen food.

  • Real-time feedback system
  • Community-driven ratings
  • Quality flagging mechanism
  • Admin action tracking
